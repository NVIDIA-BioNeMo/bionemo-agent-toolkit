# NVIDIA header logo

`nvidia-logo.svg` is the unmodified official horizontal NVIDIA logo with green eye, white lettering, and a transparent background.

Source and download: https://www.nvidia.com/content/dam/en-zz/Solutions/about-nvidia/nvidia-brochure/images/nvidia-logo-white.svg
Retrieved: 2026-10-16

The app embeds the SVG locally and preserves its proportions. NVIDIA branding remains subject to its trademark terms; it is not covered by the adapter software license.
